pylovo viewer

A read-only map of the pylovo database in your .env ({{ info?.database.dbname }} on {{ info?.database.host }}:{{ info?.database.port }}). It never writes to the database.

  • Pick a version (LV grids) and, if there is one, an MV run in the top bar.
  • One switch per level: HV grid, MV grids and LV grids. Details (the arrow) shows the single layers and their colours. Substations belong to both of their levels.
  • LV cables and buildings appear when you zoom in (from zoom 13). Regions (click a postcode or supply area, the Regions tab, or search) fly there and give the key figures.
  • Click anything for its facts and all its database attributes; hover for a quick look.
  • Each layer has a colour mode (the small menu next to it) and an i with its source.
  • 3D (on at the start) tilts the map and extrudes the buildings; with LoD2 data (citydb) the buildings in view show real roofs from zoom 15.
  • The address bar keeps the view: bookmark or share it.
